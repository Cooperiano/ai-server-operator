FAQ

Direct answers.

Is this another monitoring dashboard?

No. It is an operations layer that starts where monitoring stops: diagnosis, decision support, controlled execution, and verification.

Does the AI get root access?

No unrestricted root shell is required. Actions pass through explicit policies and human approval.

Is the free version really self-hosted?

Yes. The community edition is designed to run on your own infrastructure with your own model credentials.
